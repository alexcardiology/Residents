iPhone & iPad FREE

Install Resident Training on your Home Screen now without an Apple Developer subscription. It opens in its own app-style window and uses the same live portal and account.

Install from Safari:
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App.
  5. Tap Add. Resident Training will appear on your Home Screen.

No Apple Developer payment is needed for this free Home Screen version. We can switch the iPhone card to TestFlight/App Store later.
